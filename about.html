About College Portal

The College Portal is a modern, unified academic management solution designed to streamline student registration, fee tracking, attendance logs, and performance metrics for both students and administration.

Student Portal
Empowers students with dynamic attendance rings, fee history, and academic progress.
Admin Management
Robust administrative tools for registering students, tracking fees, and logging daily attendance.
Modern Aesthetic
Built with contemporary Bento-box layouts and glassmorphism styling for an effortless experience.
